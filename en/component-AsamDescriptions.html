Usage guidelines ​

Use title, extra, column, border, direction, and labelWidth to configure the list. Provide custom content through title, extra, and the default slot. ASAM supports the four control densities large, big, default, and small through size and inherits the surrounding size configuration. The host supplies and edits the underlying data.

The list delegates layout and slot processing to the underlying Descriptions component. It passes the slot nodes and their span and rowspan values through without cloning nodes or assigning defaults. AsamDescriptionsItem keeps the component identity the underlying list uses to recognize items. Omitted values, undefined, and null follow the underlying behavior and layout limits.
