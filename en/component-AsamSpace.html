Child size constraints ​

Space calculates its size automatically from its children’s content. Children such as Slider that depend on parent width or height need explicit container dimensions or a constrained layout; automatic sizing may otherwise fail to meet their measurement requirements. Give such children a determinable width and height before composing spacing. The following constrained slider example remains usable.

Usage conventions ​

Combine direction, alignment, wrap, fill, fillRatio, spacer and size according to their public parameters. size means spacing and accepts a number, a horizontal/vertical array or a supported spacing alias. The control size big is not a spacing alias for this entry. Place adjacent content in the default slot.
